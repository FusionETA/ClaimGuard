## 1. Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to your company's AltomateHR URL (ask your admin if you're unsure).
2. Enter your **email address** and **password**, then click **Sign In**.
3. You will be taken straight to your dashboard based on your role.

> **Forgot your password?** Contact your administrator to have it reset.

---

## 2. Understanding Your Role

AltomateHR has three types of users. Your admin will tell you which one you are when they set up your account.

| Role | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | Clock in/out, submit expense claims, view payslips |
| **Supervisor** | Everything an Employee can do, plus approve attendance and claims for your team |
| **Admin** | Manage the whole organisation — employees, settings, payroll, and reports |

---

## 3. Employee Guide

### Your Dashboard

When you log in, your **Dashboard** is the first thing you see. It gives you a quick summary of:

- **Today's attendance** — whether you're clocked in or out, and which project you're working on
- **Total reimbursed** — the year-to-date total of approved and paid expense claims
- **Claims at a glance** — how many claims are awaiting review, approved, and paid

If you are a **Supervisor**, you'll also see quick-access cards for pending attendance approvals and the claims queue.

---

### Clocking In & Out

Clocking in records the start of your working day and links your time to a project.

**To clock in:**

1. From the Dashboard, you'll see the **"Today's attendance"** section.
2. Tap or click the **Clock In** button.
3. Select the **project** you're working on from the dropdown.
4. If prompted, allow the app to access your location — this confirms you're at the right site.
5. If a selfie is required (depending on your company's policy), take a photo using your camera.
6. Tap **Confirm** to complete the clock-in.

**To clock out:**

1. From the Dashboard, tap the **Clock Out** button.
2. A summary of your working hours will appear. Review it and tap **Confirm**.
3. Your clock-out is sent to your supervisor for approval.

> **Good to know:** If you're outside the project's designated area when you clock in or out, the app will still let you proceed — but a remark will be added automatically and sent to your supervisor.

---

### Taking a Break

1. While clocked in, tap the **Break** button on your Dashboard.
2. Tap **Confirm** to start your break.
3. When you return, tap the break button again to end it.

> Your break time is excluded from your total working hours for the day.

---

### Submitting an Expense Claim

Use this whenever you've spent money for work — meals, transport, supplies, and so on — and need to be reimbursed.

**Steps:**

1. From the navigation menu, tap **Claims**.
2. Tap **New Claim** (or the **+** button on the dashboard quick actions).
3. Fill in the claim details:
   - **Category** — the type of expense (e.g. meals, fuel, accommodation). Select the one that matches your expense.
   - **Amount** — enter the amount you spent, and select the currency.
   - **Date** — the date you incurred the expense.
   - **Project** — the project this expense is related to (if applicable).
   - **Description** — a short note about what the expense was for.
   - **Receipt** — attach a photo or file of your receipt.
4. Tap **Submit**.

Your claim will move to **Awaiting Review** and your supervisor will be notified.

> **Mileage claims:** If you're claiming for driving, select the Mileage category. Enter the distance travelled — the system will calculate the amount automatically using your company's approved rate.

---

### Viewing Your Claims History

1. Go to **Claims** in the navigation menu.
2. You'll see all your past and pending claims listed here.
3. Each claim shows its current status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting Review** | Submitted, waiting for your supervisor to review |
| **Approved** | Approved by your supervisor, pending payout |
| **Paid** | Reimbursement has been processed |
| **Rejected** | Not approved — check the notes for a reason |

Tap any claim to see its full details.

---

### Viewing Your Payslips

1. Go to **Payslips** in the navigation menu.
2. Your payslips are listed by pay period.
3. Tap a payslip to view the full breakdown of your earnings and deductions.

---

### Your Account & Profile

1. Go to **Account** in the navigation menu.
2. Here you can view your personal details including your employee ID, job title, and assigned supervisor.
3. Contact your admin to update any details that need changing.

## 4. Supervisor Guide

As a supervisor, you have all the same features as an employee, plus the ability to review and approve your team's attendance and expense claims.

On your Dashboard, two extra cards appear:

- **Attendance approvals** — shows how many clock-in/out requests are waiting for you
- **Claims queue** — shows how many expense claims need your review

---

### Approving Attendance

When an employee clocks in or out, it comes to you for approval.

1. Tap the **Attendance approvals** card on the Dashboard (or go to **Attendance → Approvals** in the menu).
2. You'll see a list of pending requests. Each shows the employee's name, the action (clock in/out), the time, and the project.
3. Review the details. If everything looks correct, tap **Approve**.
4. If something seems wrong, tap **Reject** and add a note explaining why.

> **Tip:** Try to action approvals promptly — your team's working hours depend on them being confirmed.

---

### Approving Expense Claims

1. Tap the **Claims queue** card on the Dashboard (or go to **Review** in the menu).
2. You'll see all claims submitted by employees in your team.
3. Tap a claim to review it — you can see the category, amount, date, project, description, and any attached receipts.
4. Tap **Approve** to pass it through, or **Reject** if it needs to be queried. Add a note when rejecting so the employee knows why.

## 5. Admin Guide

Admins have full control over the organisation. You manage employees, set up the system, run payroll, and view reports.

---

### Admin Dashboard

When you log in as an admin, you see an **Executive Overview** — a top-level summary of the organisation's claims activity, spend by category, and overall status. This is read-only and refreshes automatically.

---

### Managing Employees & Hierarchy

Go to **Hierarchy** in the admin menu to manage your team structure.

**Adding a new employee:**

1. Go to **Hierarchy → Employees**.
2. Click **Add Employee**.
3. Fill in their name, email address, role (Employee or Supervisor), and worker type (Daily or Monthly).
4. Assign them a supervisor and a project.
5. Click **Save** — the employee will receive their login credentials by email.

**Setting up approval chains:**

AltomateHR supports flexible approval layers. You can set up who approves what for each team or project.

1. Go to **Hierarchy → Approval Chains**.
2. Set the order of approvers (e.g. Supervisor first, then Project Manager).
3. Save the chain — it will apply automatically to new requests.

---

### Reviewing Claims

1. Go to **Claims** in the admin menu.
2. You can see all claims across the organisation, filtered by status, date, or employee.
3. Use this view for reporting and oversight — individual claim approvals are handled by supervisors in their portals.

---

### Organisation Settings

Go to **Settings** to configure your organisation's rules and integrations.

**Key settings include:**

- **Working hours** — set the standard start and end times for your company (e.g. 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
- **Mileage rate** — the rate used to calculate mileage claims (e.g. RM 0.60 per km).
- **Currency** — set the default and allowed currencies for expense claims.
- **Claim policies** — configure spend limits per expense category.
- **Attendance policies** — toggle selfie requirements and geofencing (location enforcement) per employee group.
- **Xero integration** — if your company uses Xero for accounting, connect it here. This allows approved claims to be automatically pushed to Xero as bills.

---

### Payroll

Go to **Payroll** in the admin menu to manage pay runs.

1. **Review employee payroll profiles** — confirm each employee's payout method (daily rate or monthly salary) is correct.
2. **Run payroll** — create a new payroll run for the relevant period. The system will calculate based on attendance records, approved claims, and any adjustments.
3. **Review and approve** the payroll run before finalising.
4. Payslips are automatically generated and made available to employees once a run is completed.

> **Note:** Always double-check that all attendance records for the period have been approved before running payroll, as unapproved records may not be included.
